[h1]Screen Filter[/h1]
The game comes with a couple of color filters, but you can't adjust them. This mod adds a [b]Custom[/b] filter that you can tune while looking at your park, plus camera effects for a less clean, more photographic look.

[h2]Color filter[/h2]
[list]
[*]Brightness
[*]Contrast
[*]Saturation
[*]Gamma
[*]Temperature (warmer or cooler)
[*]Tint (green or magenta)
[*]Base filter: start from one of the game's own filters and adjust on top of it
[/list]

[h2]Effects[/h2]
These work with or without the color filter, and [b]Use these effects[/b] turns them all off or on at once.
[list]
[*]Vignette and its softness: darker or lighter than the game's own vignette, or removed altogether
[*]Film grain and its size
[*]Chromatic aberration: colored fringes toward the edges of the screen
[*]Bloom strength and threshold, on top of the game's own values
[/list]
At their default values, the effects leave the game's image exactly as it was.

[h2]How to use it[/h2]
[list]
[*]Open a park and press [b]J[/b] to open the window. The key can be changed in the game's controls settings.
[*]Tick [b]Use this filter[/b] to use the color filter, and move the sliders. The image changes as you drag.
[*]Close the window with [b]J[/b], [b]Esc[/b] or its [b]X[/b] button. Your values are saved and come back next time.
[*][b]Reset[/b] puts every slider back to its default. The base filter and the two checkboxes stay as they are.
[/list]
The same controls are also in the mod's settings, and Custom is listed with the other filters in the graphics options.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]It only changes the image of the park. Menus and windows stay as they are.
[*]The color filter costs about the same as one of the game's own filters. You may notice small hitches only while dragging its sliders.
[*]The effects are drawn every frame while they are on, so they use a little more of your graphics card. Grain and chromatic aberration do nothing at 0.
[*]The bloom sliders do nothing while bloom is turned off in the game's graphics options.
[*]It only affects your own screen. Other players in a multiplayer game don't need it.
[/list]

Your saves don't depend on this mod. If you remove it, the game goes back to its first filter and its own effects.
